Security

Your Data, Your Rules — No Exceptions

We built Attendance System with one clear promise: your body, your style, your data — always in your control.

Every measurement, scan, and profile you create stays strictly on your device. We don’t store it on any servers. It’s never uploaded to the cloud. And it’s never used behind your back. That means:

  • Only you can access your data.
  • We can’t see it.
  • No one else gets it.
  • No third-party sharing. Ever.

Not even our development team can peek under the hood — because there’s nothing for us to see. Your information stays locked to your phone, where it belongs.

And when you're done, you're really done. Use the "Delete Account" option to permanently remove your account and wipe every last detail — all from your device. Nothing gets saved, stored, or stashed away.

We don’t believe in fine print or shady data practices. Just full transparency and full respect for your digital self.

You came here for smart fashion. Not surveillance. And we’re keeping it that way.
